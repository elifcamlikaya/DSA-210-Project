# DSA-210-Project
# The Correlation Between Himalayan Expeditions Survival Rates and Climbers' Macroeconomic Backgrounds  

**Project Overview**

My project aims to analyze how Nations GPD affect the mountaneering climbers survival 
rate on the risky Himalayan mountains. As the mountaneering increased provided 
services are increased with it such as Sherpa help, excessive oxygen, carriage service 
etc… And all these services are quite expensive but helps the climber so much on the 
expedition. So in my project I decided to research whether a climber’s personal profile 
(age,gender, or season of climbing preference) or their economical background (by 
looking their home country’s GDP per capita) is a bigger predicator of survival on 
Himalayan expeditions. To satisfy the data enrichment I will merge individual climber 
demographic data with the global macroeconomical data and map the climbers World 
Bank income grade based on their nationality and the year of their climb. 

**Data Collection** 

I will start my Project by collecting datasets from Kaggle and also from an open source 
database called “The World Bank Open Data Portal”  which I will collect every nations 
yearly GDP. 
Both datasets will be dowloaded as CSV files. I will do the data collection and 
preparation phase using Python: pandas for data manipulation, numpy for numerical 
analysis and matplotlib and seaborn for data visualization.

**Characteristic of the Data** 

The climbers dataset I am planning to work with contains over 76,000 climbers 
records.For each climber the data contains features like age, sex, citizenship, used 
oxgyen, death cause (if passed away), succesfull summit or fail cause.  
The World Bank dataset contains historical GDP record over 260 countaries and regions 
from 1960 to 2023.
